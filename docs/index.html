Help

Pin colours

  • Inside: on the concave side, the strip bends round the pin
  • Outside: on the convex side, the pin pushes the bend
  • Straight: the strip is straight at the pin
  • Through: the strip passes through the pin centre
  • Released: a Left/Right pin the strip does not touch (listed as a warning)
  • Orange ring: the pin has a warning (see the Result status)
  • Purple ring: the selected pin
  • White dot: where the strip touches the pin
  • Red strip: bent tighter than the minimum radius
  • Red +: where the strip bends tightest (the minimum bend radius in Result)

Pin modes

  • Through (the default): the strip passes through the pin's centre, like a slotted pin or an eyelet. It slides freely and never lets go.
  • Auto: the strip touches the pin on whichever side it presses against.
  • Left / Right: the pin sits on that side of the strip, looking from pin 1 towards the last pin. It only touches when the strip presses against it, and is released otherwise.

Controls

  • Click on an empty spot to add a pin; keep the button down and drag to place it. Near the strip it goes into that stretch, otherwise onto the nearer end.
  • Drag a pin to move it. Alt turns off grid snapping.
  • Right-click a pin, or press Delete, to remove it.
  • Double-click a pin or press X to cycle Through → Auto → Left → Right; T sets Through, A sets Auto.
  • Arrows nudge one minor grid step, Shift one major step.
  • Wheel zooms. Pan by dragging with the middle or right button, on a ruler, or with two fingers. F fits the work area; pressed again, it fits the pins.
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo, Esc deselects.
